## FAQ

**Q: Can I use the open-source version commercially?**
A: Yes. Apache 2.0 and CERN-OHL-P v2 both permit commercial use. You only need a
commercial license if you want access to proprietary components or require additional
legal protections.

**Q: Can I build a product on top of this platform and sell it?**
A: Yes, the open-source licenses explicitly allow this. Attribution is required.

**Q: What's the difference between the open-source and commercial versions?**
A: The open-source version includes the complete platform (firmware, ROS2 stack, SDK,
hardware designs). The commercial license adds proprietary AI models, cloud management
services, an advanced web dashboard, and enterprise support.
